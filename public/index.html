How to install

  1. Download & unzip

    Once available, download Cally above and unzip the file. Keep the extracted folder on your computer.

  2. Open Chrome extensions

    Enter chrome://extensions in your address bar and turn on Developer mode.

  3. Load Cally

    Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.

  4. Open your chart

    Visit a pump.fun or Terminal coin page and allow site access if prompted. Cally appears on the chart.
