How to install

  1. Download & unzip

    Download Cally above and unzip the file. Keep the extracted folder on your computer.

  2. Open Chrome extensions

    Enter chrome://extensions in your address bar and turn on Developer mode.

  3. Load Cally

    Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.

  4. Open your chart

    Review the setup page and click Agree & enable Cally. Then open a pump.fun or Terminal coin page.
