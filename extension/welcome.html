cally.

Cally reads the coin’s linked website and X posts on supported charts. When you open its popup, it reads the active page or your selected text.

To generate a draft, source text, coin context and relevant public tweet images are sent to Cally’s hosted service and OpenAI. Coin addresses are sent to LarpCheck for ratings. Cally stores an installation ID for usage limits and drafts locally in your browser.

Review drafts before posting. Cally never trades, signs wallet transactions or posts for you.
